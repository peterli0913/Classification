Results

What the numbers establish

Interpolation is already strong

Kraken XGBoost R² 0.857 (RMSE 0.0182) on buried-volume ratio. BH multi-FP XGB random 5-fold R² 0.938 (RMSE 6.77). Doyle reaction-0 bootstrap R² 0.932 (RMSE 7.84).

Transfer is chemistry-dependent

BH leave-one-ligand mean R² is only 0.36 because XPhos fails (R² ≈ −0.92). BrettPhos family transfers (multi-FP R² 0.94 / 0.90). Multi-FP lowers RMSE on 17/22 unseen additives.

Trees beat graphs; intervals are calibrated

Ligand GIN R² 0.185 vs tree R² 0.94 (four unique ligand graphs). Split conformal 80/90% coverage is 0.796 / 0.891. Retrospective UCB ends at 84.3 vs random 81.8 (pool max 86.6).

Limits that are also results

What is established, and where the models stop

Established

  • Kraken XGBoost R² 0.857 on vbur_ratio_vbur_vtot
  • BH multi-FP interpolation R² 0.938; additive transfer 17/22
  • BrettPhos-family LOLO works; XPhos and Xantphos do not
  • Split conformal is empirically calibrated
  • UCB beats random in an 8-seed retrospective pool

Model limits

  • The Kraken AF label is a buried-volume ratio, not yield
  • GNN R² is −0.45 on reaction 0 and 0.08–0.19 on the full table
  • Dashboard scaffold RMSE 14.19 is the honest 790-row number
  • Discrete BO ranks existing condition tuples
  • Suzuki area% and BH isolated yield are not the same physical quantity
